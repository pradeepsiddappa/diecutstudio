# Panda Treat Box

Animal-box family member. **Same body, lid, fold animation and printable-face
pipeline as the [Bunny Treat Box](bunny-box.md)** — see the
**[animal-boxes playbook](../animal-boxes.md)**. Only the ears and face differ.

- **Model:** `src/core/models/pandaBox.ts` (spec) on `animalBox.ts` · **id:** `panda-box`
- **Category:** party · **Status:** built (folds + opens/closes in 3D)

## What's different from the bunny

- **Ears:** round dome ears (like the bear), filled **black**.
- **Face (5 recolourable groups):** `fur` (the two black ear fills + two black
  eye patches), `eyes` (white discs in the patches), `pupils` (dark discs),
  `nose` (a dark oval), `mouth` (a stroked smile). Order: fur → eyes → pupils so
  the white eyes layer onto the black patches.

Everything else is the family default.
