# Bat Treat Box

Animal-box family member (Halloween). **Same body, lid, fold animation and
printable-face pipeline as the [Bunny Treat Box](bunny-box.md)** — see the
**[animal-boxes playbook](../animal-boxes.md)**. Only the ears and face differ.

- **Model:** `src/core/models/batBox.ts` (spec) on `animalBox.ts` · **id:** `bat-box`
- **Category:** party · **Status:** built (folds + opens/closes in 3D)

## What's different from the bunny

- **Ears:** two pointed bat ears at the corners.
- **Face (5 recolourable groups):** `face` (a dark rounded head patch + dark fills
  on the two ears), `eyes` (big light discs), `pupils` (dark), `mouth` (a stroked
  smile), `fangs` (two little white triangles). Order: face → eyes → pupils.

Everything else is the family default.
