Frequently asked questions

What is a dieline?
A dieline is the flat, unfolded outline of a box showing where to cut (the trim) and where to score or fold (the creases). Die Cut Studio generates dielines parametrically from the dimensions you type.
Is Die Cut Studio free?
Yes — it runs in your browser with no signup or watermark, and the dielines you generate are yours for personal and commercial use.
What file formats can I export?
PDF, SVG and DXF, all at true 1:1 scale so they print and cut ruler-accurate.
Does it work with Cricut and Silhouette?
Yes — export SVG (Cricut Design Space, Silhouette Designer Edition) or DXF (free Silhouette edition). Blue lines cut, red lines score.
